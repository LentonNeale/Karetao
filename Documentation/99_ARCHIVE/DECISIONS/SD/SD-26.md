# SD-26 — Differential end-effector motor/DOF mapping

**Status:** PROPOSED

## Summary

The two end-effector motors drive two end-effector rotational DOFs through a differential mechanism. The mapping must be represented explicitly rather than as two independent one-to-one joints.

## Open Issue / Comment

Define the forward/inverse transformation, motor signs, gear ratios, zero offsets, joint limits, singular/invalid configurations, and representation in ros2_control and MoveIt 2. This remains open until the mechanical model and mapping are verified.
