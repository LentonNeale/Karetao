# System Requirements

## Authority

This document is the authoritative Karetao system-requirement destination. Requirement statements are reconciled from the approved or modified Karetao system decisions, the Issue #12 clock-synchronization decisions ([`ISSUE_12_CLOCK_SYNCHRONIZATION_DECISIONS.md`](../07_DECISIONS/ISSUE_12_CLOCK_SYNCHRONIZATION_DECISIONS.md)), the project charter ([`PROJECT_CHARTER.md`](PROJECT_CHARTER.md)) and system-facing contracts. Each statement retains source provenance; decision records remain authoritative for decision status and rationale. Lenton Neale approves changes to this document.

Numeric values live in the parameter register ([`PARAMETER_REGISTER.md`](../06_CONFIGURATION/PARAMETER_REGISTER.md)) with a basis; requirements reference them by PRM ID and carry no numbers of their own (the SPI frame constants in SYS-SPI-001 are contract constants, not tunable values).

## Baseline (charter scope, 2026-10-03)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| SYS-ENC-001 | The Arm Sensor Hub shall read every encoder recorded as fitted in the commissioning table ([`SYSTEM_CONFIGURATION.md`](../06_CONFIGURATION/SYSTEM_CONFIGURATION.md) §2) and publish each reading in the snapshot with its acquisition timestamp and validity flags. Acceptance: on the bench, every fitted axis shows a valid reading that changes when the joint is moved, with a timestamp from the STM32 local clock. | Charter goal 1 | Approved (charter) 2026-10-03 |
| SYS-ENC-002 | An axis recorded as not fitted shall be published as not fitted (validity bit 6, `SPI_PAYLOAD_SCHEMA.md`), shall not be reported as a fault, shall not raise a health or lifecycle fault, and shall not inhibit any other behaviour. Acceptance: with an axis marked not fitted, the snapshot shows NOT_FITTED for that axis, the health monitor reports no sensor fault for it, and the other axes publish normally. | Charter goal 1 | Approved (charter) 2026-10-03; wire format per D-10 implemented; behaviour test outstanding (GitHub #66) |
| SYS-ENC-003 | The commissioning table in [`SYSTEM_CONFIGURATION.md`](../06_CONFIGURATION/SYSTEM_CONFIGURATION.md) §2 is the only statement of which axes carry an encoder, which mux channel each uses and which motor each maps to; it is versioned, and the firmware tables in `app_init.c` implement it and must match it. Acceptance: the table and `app_init.c` agree at every merge (checked by inspection; a later tool may automate it). | Charter goal 1; SD-07 (sensor half of SYS-MAP-001) | Approved (charter) 2026-10-03 |
| SYS-MOT-001 | The snapshot shall carry the shaft position of every motor registered in the commissioning table ([`SYSTEM_CONFIGURATION.md`](../06_CONFIGURATION/SYSTEM_CONFIGURATION.md) §2.1), at rest and during motion, each motor record with its acquisition timestamp and validity flags. Acceptance: on the bench with all registered motors powered, every motor record is valid and its position follows commanded motion; with one motor unpowered, only that record goes stale. | Charter goal 2; SD-08 | Approved (charter) 2026-10-03; today one motor is registered |
| SYS-MOT-002 | Motor feedback that stops arriving shall be detected within the timeout in PRM-MOT-01 and handled per PRM-MOT-02 (degraded, then inhibit and FAULT); the last valid payload is kept and marked stale, never overwritten by a failed decode. Acceptance: with a motor's feedback interrupted, the record goes stale within PRM-MOT-01 and the escalation in PRM-MOT-02 is observed in diagnostics. | SD-04 | Approved |
| SYS-TS-001 | Every sensor and motor record shall carry its own acquisition timestamp taken from the STM32 local monotonic clock immediately after the sample is received; the STM32 performs no clock math; the Pi converts timestamps through ClockSync; the skew between the records of one snapshot shall be derivable from those timestamps, with PRM-TS-02 as the working target and PRM-TS-01 as the conversion-error target. Acceptance: a snapshot decoded on the Pi yields per-record timestamps whose spread matches the measured sweep (PRM-ACQ-02) and that convert through ClockSync when it is valid. | Charter goal 2; SD-08, SD-18; Issue #12 item 7 | Approved (charter) 2026-10-03; Pi-side conversion not yet implemented (GitHub #42) |
| SYS-LINK-001 | Raspberry Pi communication loss shall be detected by a count-based liveness check on the STM32: `missed_count` increments once per expected-message interval without an accepted, valid SPI message and resets on any accepted, valid message; `link_stale` trips at `missed_count >= N_moving` while moving or `>= N_idle` while idle (`N_moving < N_idle`). On trip, motion commands shall be rejected and the STM32 motion gate shall fail closed; `link_stale` clears on the next accepted, valid message. Until `N_moving`/`N_idle` (PRM-LINK-02) are set, detection shall occur within PRM-LINK-01. Acceptance: with the Pi stopped, `link_stale` is set within PRM-LINK-01 and a SET_MOTOR_COMMAND is NACKed; on the next valid message `link_stale` clears. | SD-03; Issue #12 item 11 (ex SYS-FAIL-001) | Modified; `N_moving`/`N_idle`, expected-interval source and motion-state input open |
| SYS-LINK-002 | On Raspberry Pi communication loss, the system shall perform a controlled stop where possible and then motor disable; it shall never hold the last motion command. Acceptance: with a motor moving and the Pi link cut, the motor is commanded to a controlled stop and then disabled, and no earlier move command is still being executed. | SD-03 (ex SYS-FAIL-002) | Approved; stop timing remains to be measured |
| SYS-CAN-001 | The CAN network shall use standard 11-bit identifiers at the bitrate in PRM-CAN-01 with average load below the ceiling in PRM-CAN-04. CAN bus-off shall inhibit commands, perform bounded recovery, escalate after repeated failure, and require explicit reactivation. Acceptance: forcing a bus-off inhibits motor commands, recovery is attempted at the delays in PRM-CAN-07, repeated bus-off escalates per PRM-CAN-08, and commands resume only after explicit reactivation; bitrate and load are checked in the Stage 3 measurement. | SD-05, SD-06 (ex SYS-CAN-003; absorbs SYS-CAN-001/002) | Approved (bus-off); bitrate and load Modified, target-hardware validation required |
| SYS-MAP-001 | Logical-joint and motor/CAN-ID mapping shall be deterministic and versioned, including direction, zero offset and transmission ratio (the sensor half is in SYS-ENC-003). Acceptance: the mapping is a single versioned table in the commissioning configuration, and a change of direction, zero offset or ratio is visible as a table version change. | SD-07 | Approved |
| SYS-CAL-001 | Calibration shall be computed on the Pi/workstation and transferred to the STM32 through a controlled validated configuration path. Calibration records shall be versioned, CRC-protected, redundant in STM32 flash, power-loss protected, and have a host-side source copy. Acceptance: a calibration record loaded to the STM32 reports its version; a corrupted copy is detected by CRC and the redundant copy is used; interrupting power during a write leaves a valid record; the host source copy matches the loaded version. | SD-10, SD-11 | Approved; evidence required (versioning, CRC, redundancy, power-loss, host copy) |
| SYS-IF-001 | Calibrated radians shall be authoritative at the host/API; wire-schema changes shall be explicitly versioned. Joint state shall include stable identity, position, available velocity, validity/status, acquisition timestamp, synchronization quality and calibration version (SYS-ENC-001 gives the per-axis sensor record content). Acceptance: the host API returns positions in radians with each of those fields present, and any wire-schema change is accompanied by a schema version change in `SPI_PAYLOAD_SCHEMA.md`. | SD-12, SD-13 (absorbs SYS-STATE-001) | Modified |
| SYS-SPI-001 | SPI transactions shall use a 512-byte frame, maximum 490-byte payload, Mode 0, little-endian fields, CRC-32/ISO-HDLC and deterministic request/response ownership. Correlated GET_SNAPSHOT responses take priority, active DMA buffers remain immutable, and replacement frames use replace-if-new semantics. DATA_READY may provide latency notification and polling (period PRM-SPI-03, Pi as SPI clock master) provides recovery. Acceptance: the C and Python protocol tests exchange the same frames bit-for-bit, a corrupted CRC is rejected, and with DATA_READY unavailable the Pi still receives snapshots by polling. | SD-15, SD-16, SD-17 (absorbs SYS-SPI-002/003) | Approved; state-age impact requires validation; physical DATA_READY assignment unresolved |
| SYS-CMD-001 | The host command interface shall provide single gated commands (enable, disable, controlled stop, move to position, move at velocity) per motor through SET_MOTOR_COMMAND; the hub answers ACK when the gate accepts and queues the command to CAN, NACK with the gate reason otherwise; ACK does not mean executed, completion is observed in the snapshot motor record; timeouts per PRM-LINK-01. Acceptance: each command type sent to a motor with the gate open is ACKed and its effect appears in the snapshot motor record; with the gate closed it is NACKed with the gate reason. | SD-14 | Approved 2026-10-03; payload frozen in SPI_PAYLOAD_SCHEMA.md; not yet implemented (GitHub #63) |
| SYS-GATE-001 | Normal motor motion shall be permitted only while the motion gate holds: lifecycle ACTIVE, CAN ACTIVE, Pi link not stale (SYS-LINK-001), no global motion inhibit, motor ready. Loss of any input fails closed (new commands rejected, controlled stop then disable per SYS-LINK-002); recovery requires explicit reactivation (SYS-REC-001). Acceptance: with each input removed in turn, a SET_MOTOR_COMMAND is NACKed with that input's reason code; with all present it is ACKed. | [`ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md`](../07_DECISIONS/ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md) (D-3) | Approved 2026-10-03 (confirm at sign-off); code evaluates a superset today |
| SYS-REC-001 | Persistent, repeated or freshness failures shall enter ERROR; bounded recovery shall return to INACTIVE. A system restart shall return to INACTIVE. Reactivation shall be explicit and dependency-ordered, and the system shall never automatically resume motor motion. Acceptance: a forced persistent failure reaches ERROR; recovery or a restart lands in INACTIVE with motion rejected until each dependency is reactivated in order; no motor moves without a new command after recovery or restart. | SD-23, SD-24 (merges SYS-REC-001/002) | Approved; system-level motion gating during recovery requires explicit definition |
| SYS-SYNC-001 | Every applicable SPI response shall be a ClockSync observation: the request `seq` is the exchange ID and the response carries `request_received_local_us` (versioned payload extension) captured from the raw TIM2 counter in the SPI DMA-complete ISR and extended to 64 bits in task context. The Pi-side ClockSync model estimates offset and frequency; the STM32 performs no clock math. Synchronization becomes invalid after PRM-TS-03 without an accepted observation, and an STM32 reset invalidates the model until new accepted observations make it valid again. Implausible observations are rejected without silent slew by ClockSync, which is the single acceptance policy (a legacy drift check may remain only as a secondary diagnostic and does not modify ClockSync state). `link_stale` and `ClockSync.is_valid()` are independent: `link_stale` gates STM32 motion permission, `ClockSync.is_valid()` governs timestamp conversion; when ClockSync is invalid, acquisition continues and timestamps are marked unsynchronized. The timestamp error target is PRM-TS-01. Acceptance: with SPI traffic flowing, the Pi ClockSync becomes valid; stopping traffic invalidates it after PRM-TS-03; an STM32 reset invalidates it; an implausible observation is rejected with ClockSync state unchanged; conversion error is measured against PRM-TS-01 in Stage 3. | SD-18, SD-19, SD-21, SD-22; Issue #12 items 1-7, 9 | Modified; numeric bounds require commissioning validation |

Safety behaviours SAFE-01..05 are in [`04_SAFETY/SAFETY_REQUIREMENTS.md`](../04_SAFETY/SAFETY_REQUIREMENTS.md).
